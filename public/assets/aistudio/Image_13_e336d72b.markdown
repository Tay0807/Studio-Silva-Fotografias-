## Brand & Style

### Personality & Narrative
This design system embodies the warmth, emotional gravity, and timeless heritage of an intimate family photography studio rooted in regional authenticity. It is neither a detached luxury brand nor a clinical tech tool; rather, it communicates the tactility of fine art albums, archival heirloom prints, and gentle natural lighting. The voice is welcoming, respectful, and deeply human, treating family portraits, weddings, and generational milestones with quiet dignity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
The experience is designed for families, couples, and individuals seeking meaningful, generational portraiture. Interacting with the digital experience should evoke comfort, nostalgia, trust, and creative reverence. Content must feel breathable and unhurried—allowing the imagery to carry emotional weight without visual noise or aggressive conversion patterns.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
The aesthetic blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Fine-Art Sensibilities**:
- **Generous Canvas:** Significant breathing room around photographic compositions and text blocks.
- **Editorial Proportion:** Classic typographic contrasts reminiscent of independent art monographs and archival journals.
- **Organic Subtlety:** Soft borders inspired by deckle-edge paper and subtle surface shifts, avoiding harsh digital contrasts.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette relies entirely on organic, paper-inspired foundational tones paired with warm earth pigments:

- **Primary (`#9E7250`):** Muted terracotta/warm ochre. Used exclusively for deliberate emphasis—editorial accents, refined call-to-actions, and moments of intimate celebration.
- **Secondary (`#8C827A`):** Warm taupe. Serves structural duties such as secondary metadata, discreet captions, borders, and passive iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#A69C93`):** Soft stone grey-brown. Employed for low-contrast borders, hairline separators, and muted hover states.
- **Neutral (`#232220`):** Deep graphite with a warm undertone. Replaces pure black to retain soft readability and human warmth across typography and high-emphasis elements.

### Surface Tones & Canvases
- **Primary Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Soft ivory/off-white base creating an inviting, paper-like ground for photographic collections.
- **Secondary Canvas & Containers (`#EFEAE1`):** Gentle sand/linen hue for card backgrounds, elevated framing, and grouped narrative blocks.
- **Surface Variant (`#E5DFD3`):** Deeper sand for nested containers, table headers, and structural resting points.
- **Text Secondary (`#363432`):** Slightly softened graphite for continuous long-form body copy.

## Typography

### Hierarchy & Pairings
- **Display & Headings (Newsreader):** Brings poetic authority, warmth, and literary weight. Headings are set with generous vertical tracking and natural letterforms, allowing titles to read like book chapter intros or gallery placards.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides an open, humanist aperture with warm terminals that match the empathy of the photography, guaranteeing effortless legibility across dense pricing guides, booking schedules, and mobile viewing.
- **Editorial Accents (`quote`):** Styled with Newsreader in italic styling when used for testimonials or emotional context behind photo sessions.

### Application Rules
- Maintain an open line height (`1.5` to `1.65`) for all running body copy to ensure a calm, relaxed reading pace.
- Avoid all-caps transformations on serif headlines; reserve uppercase strictly for `label-sm` category badges or metadata with increased letter spacing (`0.05em`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Compositional Rhythm
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (max-width `1280px` centered), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.
- **Asymmetric Editorial Groupings:** Layouts favor asymmetrical alignments (e.g., a 7-column hero image paired with a 5-column story block) to simulate an editorial art book layout rather than a rigid dashboard structure.
- **Visual Breathing Room:** Margins expand aggressively on widescreen viewports (`3rem` to `4.5rem`) to frame photo series with museum-like calm.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Stacked photo narratives; single-column forms and package reviews.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Balanced two-column portfolio grids and split card presentations.
- **Desktop (> 1025px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`. Expansive photo spreads, side-by-side session comparisons, and negative-space borders.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification
Depth is communicated primarily through deliberate surface color shifting rather than harsh drop shadows. Components emerge naturally from the ivory canvas (`#FAF8F5`) onto soft sand layers (`#EFEAE1`), giving the visual sensation of layered archival card stock.

### Ambient Shadows
When physical separation is required (such as interactive modals, elevated package cards, or floating appointment summaries), shadows must remain warm, diffused, and low in contrast:
- **Floating Surface:** `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(54, 52, 50, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(54, 52, 50, 0.03)`
- **Hover Transition:** `0 14px 40px -6px rgba(54, 52, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(54, 52, 50, 0.04)`

### Hairline Boundaries
Where borders are introduced, they should never utilize pure grey or black. Hairlines use `1px solid #A69C93` at `35%` opacity or `#E5DFD3` at full opacity to mimic refined bookbinding seams.

## Shapes

### Edge Philosophy
With a roundedness level of `2`, the design system achieves an organic, tactile presence. Sharp 90-degree corners are softened to remove digital rigidity, while avoiding the overly bubbly look of hyper-playful consumer apps.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`):** Buttons, interactive fields, chips, and small thumbnails.
- **`rounded-lg` (`1rem`):** Editorial cards, package summaries, and secondary narrative containers.
- **`rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`):** Featured imagery frames, modal containers, and hero composition panels.
- **Pill Shapes:** Strictly reserved for standalone status tags and metadata chips.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Button:** Solid muted terracotta (`#9E7250`) background with clean white typography (`#FAF8F5`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Border radius: `0.5rem`. Smooth color shift to `#896041` on hover.
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined boundary with `1px solid #8C827A` over transparent canvas, text set in graphite (`#232220`). Hover shifts background to `#EFEAE1`.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Understated inline text link accompanied by a subtle bottom border (`1px solid #9E7250`) with `0.25rem` offset, evoking handwritten notes in catalog margins.

### Cards & Image Frames
- **Portfolio & Package Cards:** Built on sand surfaces (`#EFEAE1`), encased in a subtle border (`#E5DFD3`), with `1rem` radius. Padding set to `1.5rem`. Images within cards carry matching upper radii and maintain an aspect ratio of 3:2 or 4:5.
- **Framed Photographic Presentation:** Images may feature an inset paper margin (`0.75rem` of `#FAF8F5`) surrounding the visual, recreating museum-matted archival prints.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Generous interior padding (`0.875rem 1rem`). Background set to `#FAF8F5` against `#EFEAE1` containers or solid `#FFFFFF` on `#FAF8F5`. Border: `1px solid #A69C93` at 50% opacity. Focused states shift border to `#9E7250` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(158, 114, 80, 0.15)`).
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md` using Plus Jakarta Sans, placed cleanly above inputs with `#8C827A` tone.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom circular radios and rounded square checkboxes (`0.25rem` radius). 
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #8C827A`. Checked: solid `#9E7250` with a pure ivory check icon or center dot.

### Chips & Session Badges
- Used for photography categories (e.g., "Ensaio Família", "Gestante", "Batizado", "Retrato Atemporal").
- Background: `#FAF8F5` with a delicate border of `#E5DFD3`. Typography: `label-sm` in `#8C827A`.

### Lists & Archival Metadata
- Information lists (investment summaries, session preparation steps) utilize fine hairline dividers (`#E5DFD3`).
- List items feature generous vertical padding (`1rem 0`) with custom warm terracotta dot markers or small classic Roman numeral counters.
